Nearwise on the car display

Mock-ups of the car screens, drawn from the list-and-map layouts that Android Auto and CarPlay use. Car screens do not show the phone card: they show a list of places beside a map, with the main actions at the bottom. Photos and the full text are left out. Actual rendering depends on the car and on each platform's approval rules.
