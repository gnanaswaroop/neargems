## What the app does

Near Gems suggests places near where you are, such as restaurants, cafés, bars and landmarks, with
distance, direction and rough walking and driving times. It can read a summary aloud.

## Estimates, not directions

Distances, directions, and walking and driving times are estimates. The app does not give routes and
does not know about road closures, traffic or opening hours. Check the place before you go, and use
your own navigation for directions.
